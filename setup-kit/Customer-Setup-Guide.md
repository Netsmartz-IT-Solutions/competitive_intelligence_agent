# Customer Setup Guide

## Objective
Prepare a Microsoft 365 tenant for the Competitive Intelligence agent so teams can analyze competitors, track market changes, and generate battlecards.

## Steps
1. Review the agent scope and confirm it is limited to competitive intelligence workflows.
2. Add approved source locations such as SharePoint sites and OneDrive folders for competitor profiles, win/loss notes, pricing books, and market memos.
3. Seed the workspace with at least one battlecard, one competitor profile, and one recent win/loss note.
4. Share the agent with business stakeholders in sales, strategy, product, and marketing.
5. Test prompts such as "Update Battlecard" and "Win/Loss Analysis" using internal documents.

## Recommended Content Sources
- Competitive Intelligence library
- Strategy and market insights SharePoint sites
- Pricing and positioning documents
- Recent competitor announcements
